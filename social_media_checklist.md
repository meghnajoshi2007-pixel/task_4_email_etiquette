# Social Media Do’s and Don’ts for College Students

## ✅ Do’s

- Maintain a professional profile photo and bio on platforms like LinkedIn.
- Think before posting — ensure your content reflects positively on you.
- Verify information before sharing news or updates.
- Use privacy settings to control who can see your posts.
- Communicate respectfully in comments and messages.
- Showcase achievements, certifications, and academic projects professionally.

## ❌ Don’ts

- Do not share personal information like OTPs, banking details, or home address.
- Avoid posting offensive, inappropriate, or discriminatory content.
- Do not engage in online arguments or cyberbullying.
- Avoid criticizing professors, classmates, or institutions publicly.
- Do not post content that could harm your future academic or career opportunities.
- Avoid oversharing personal problems or sensitive details publicly.
